# Brand Guidelines

Name: Harbour and Hearth Coffee Roasters. Colours: deep harbour blue, warm brick red and cream. Typeface: a serif for headings, a clean sans for everything else.

Voice: warm, plain-spoken, a little dry. We say "coffee", not "beverage". We never say "artisanal".

The bags were redesigned in 2026 by a local studio for 2,400; files live in the shared drive. Printing and stock in [[Packaging Supplier]]. Social posts follow [[Social Media Plan]].
