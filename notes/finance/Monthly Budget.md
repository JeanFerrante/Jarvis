# Monthly Budget

Average monthly revenue in 2026 is about 46,000: café 29,000, wholesale 11,000, retail and online 6,000, which includes the [[Subscription Program]].

Main costs per month: labour about 15,600 (34 percent, see [[Staff Schedule and Pay]]), green coffee about 5,200 (see [[Green Coffee Costs]]), rent 3,800, milk and pastries about 4,100, packaging 600, utilities 900, the machine service contract 120, marketing 450.

Net margin runs at about 8 percent. The goal in [[Annual Goals 2027]] is 11 percent, mostly by growing wholesale, which has the best margin.
