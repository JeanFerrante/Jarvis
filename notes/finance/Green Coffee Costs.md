# Green Coffee Costs

Green coffee is our biggest cost after labour and rent. In 2026 we paid on average 7.40 per kg landed, up from 6.65 in 2025, an 11 percent rise driven by the Brazil harvest and freight.

The House Espresso costs about 2.10 in green coffee per 250 g bag after roast loss, and sells for 11.50 retail. The [[Kivu Direct Trade]] lot costs more but sells at 14.00.

We raised retail prices by 0.50 in May to cover it, see [[Menu and Pricing]]. Importer terms in [[Andes Green Coffee Importers]]. Budget impact in [[Monthly Budget]].
