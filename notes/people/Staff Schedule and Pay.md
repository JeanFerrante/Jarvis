# Staff Schedule and Pay

Six staff plus the owner. The café runs two baristas on weekdays and three on Saturday and Sunday mornings.

Base pay is 14.20 an hour for baristas, 16.50 for the lead barista ([[Sam Reid]]), 17.00 for the assistant roaster ([[Tom Okafor]], rising to 18.50 in January), and salaried for [[Priya Shah]] and [[Maria Lopez]]. Tips are pooled and split by hours weekly.

The rota is published two weeks ahead every Thursday. Labour runs at about 34 percent of revenue, see [[Monthly Budget]]. The target is 32.
