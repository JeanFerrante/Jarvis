# Onboarding Checklist

For every new hire, written by [[Maria Lopez]]:

1. Paperwork and bank details on day one.
2. Shadow an opening and a closing shift, see [[Opening and Closing Procedures]].
3. Espresso dial-in and milk training with [[Sam Reid]] in week one.
4. Join a [[Quality Control Cupping]] session within two weeks.
5. Read the [[Cash Handling Policy]] and do a supervised till count.
6. Learn the [[Menu and Pricing]] and the allergen sheet.

New hires are on a four-week trial. Feedback conversation at the end of week two and week four.
