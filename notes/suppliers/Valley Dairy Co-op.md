# Valley Dairy Co-op

Local dairy cooperative, 20 miles away. Delivers whole and semi-skimmed milk daily at 6:45. We use about 70 litres a day on weekdays and 110 on weekends.

Price is 1.12 a litre, fixed until March 2027. Oat milk comes separately from the cash and carry because the co-op does not stock it.

If the delivery is late, call the depot first; the number is in [[Supplier Contacts]]. Daily orders are part of [[Inventory Management]].
