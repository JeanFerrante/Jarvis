# Bakery Partnership

Pastries come from Crumb and Co., delivered by 7:10 daily. We pay wholesale and keep a 62 percent margin.

In September we found we were throwing away 9 percent of pastries, mostly on Mondays and Tuesdays. We cut the Monday order by a third and waste dropped to 4 percent. See [[Inventory Management]].

The almond croissant is the top seller, then the cardamom bun. A seasonal mince pie joins in December, see [[Holiday Season Plan]]. Prices on [[Menu and Pricing]].
