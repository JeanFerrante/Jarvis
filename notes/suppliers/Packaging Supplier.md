# Packaging Supplier

Retail bags, one-way valves and labels come from Greenleaf Pack. 250 g compostable kraft bags cost 0.41 each with the valve; minimum order 2,000.

Labels are printed in-house on the thermal printer. The bag design was refreshed in 2026 to match the [[Brand Guidelines]].

Lead time is three weeks, so the order for the [[Holiday Season Plan]] gift boxes must go in by 10 November. Contact in [[Supplier Contacts]].
