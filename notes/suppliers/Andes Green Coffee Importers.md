# Andes Green Coffee Importers

Our main green coffee importer for Colombia, Peru and Brazil. Account rep is Lucía Ferrer. Payment terms are net 30.

We buy about 1,800 kg a year from them, mostly the Brazil Cerrado for the House Espresso and a washed Huila Colombia for the Night Shift blend.

Prices went up 11 percent in 2026 because of the harvest; see [[Green Coffee Costs]]. Orders over 600 kg get free freight. Contact details are in [[Supplier Contacts]]; stock levels in [[Inventory Management]].
