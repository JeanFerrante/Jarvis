# Kivu Direct Trade

A small direct-trade partner working with washing stations around Lake Kivu in Rwanda. We buy two lots a year, about 400 kg, at 6.80 per kg FOB, well above the commodity price.

Their coffee is our best-scoring single origin, 87 points at our last [[Quality Control Cupping]], and it leads the [[Subscription Program]] every spring.
