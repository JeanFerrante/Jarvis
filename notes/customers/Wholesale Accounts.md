# Wholesale Accounts

14 wholesale accounts: 9 cafés, 3 restaurants, an office and a bookshop. They buy about 220 kg a month between them, mostly the Night Shift blend, which [[Priya Shah]] designed.

Wholesale price is 24 per kg for the blends. [[Tom Okafor]] delivers on Tuesdays and Fridays. New accounts get free barista training and a grinder on loan.

The biggest account is The Long Table restaurant at 40 kg a month. We lost one café in August when it closed. Target is 20 accounts in [[Annual Goals 2027]]. Roasting for wholesale happens on Fridays, see [[Roasting Schedule]].
