# Community Events

A latte art throwdown on the last Friday of every month, run by [[Sam Reid]]: 10 entry, winner takes the pot and a bag of coffee. Usually 12 to 16 baristas from around town.

A public cupping on the first Saturday of the month at 10:00, led by [[Priya Shah]], free, maximum 12 people, booked through Instagram, see [[Social Media Plan]].

Events bring in new wholesale leads: two of our [[Wholesale Accounts]] started from the throwdown.
