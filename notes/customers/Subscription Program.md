# Subscription Program

260 active subscribers as of October 2026. Options: one or two 250 g bags every two or four weeks, roaster's choice single origin or the House Espresso.

Price is 12.50 a bag including delivery. Churn is about 6 percent a month; most cancellations say "too much coffee", so we added the four-week option in July and churn dropped from 9 percent.

Subscription coffee is roasted on Wednesdays, see [[Roasting Schedule]]. Each bag carries tasting notes from [[Quality Control Cupping]]. Target is 400 subscribers in [[Annual Goals 2027]]. The gift subscription is the centrepiece of the [[Holiday Season Plan]].
