# Seasonal Drinks

Autumn 2026: maple oat latte (4.20) and a spiced cascara tonic (3.90). The maple latte outsold every seasonal drink we have ever done, about 60 a day in October.

Winter, from 1 December: gingerbread flat white (4.10) and the Christmas blend as a filter, see [[Holiday Season Plan]].

Rule of thumb: seasonal drinks must use ingredients we already stock and take under 90 seconds to make, or the Saturday queue gets worse, see [[Customer Feedback Log]]. Prices sit alongside [[Menu and Pricing]].
