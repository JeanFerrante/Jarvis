# Opening and Closing Procedures

Opening (7:00, café opens 7:30): turn on the espresso machine first, it needs 25 minutes to stabilise. Dial in the House Espresso: 18 g in, 38 g out, 28 to 32 seconds. Fill the pastry case from the [[Bakery Partnership]] delivery, which arrives by 7:10. Count the till float: 150.

Closing (18:00): backflush, clean the steam wands, empty the grinders' hoppers into airtight tubs, wipe down and mop. Cash goes into the safe; the day's sales are reconciled against the POS, see [[Cash Handling Policy]].

Whoever closes sends a one-line message to the team chat: what ran out and anything broken.
