# Roast Log Standards

Every batch gets a line in the roast log: date, origin, green weight, roasted weight, charge temperature, first crack time, development time, drop temperature and the roaster's initials.

Target weight loss is 14 to 16 percent for espresso and 12 to 14 percent for filter. Anything outside that range gets cupped the next morning before it can be sold. [[Priya Shah]] reviews the log every Thursday.

We keep the log in the shared spreadsheet, not on paper; the paper log got coffee spilled on it twice in 2025. Cupping notes from the [[Quality Control Cupping]] session are linked by batch number.
