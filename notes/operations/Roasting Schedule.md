# Roasting Schedule

We roast on Monday, Wednesday and Friday mornings, starting at 6:30 before the café opens. [[Priya Shah]] runs the roaster on Mondays and Wednesdays; [[Tom Okafor]] covers Fridays.

- Monday: espresso blends (House Espresso and Night Shift), about 60 kg.
- Wednesday: single origins for the retail shelf and the [[Subscription Program]], about 45 kg.
- Friday: wholesale orders for [[Wholesale Accounts]], plus whatever the café ran low on.

Each batch is 12 kg on the Diedrich IR-12. Every batch gets logged in [[Roast Log Standards]]. Green beans come from [[Andes Green Coffee Importers]] and [[Kivu Direct Trade]]. If the roaster is down, see [[Equipment Maintenance]].
