# Cash Handling Policy

Till float is 150 every morning. Two people count at close whenever possible. Discrepancies over 10 get written in the shift log with an explanation.

Cash is banked every Tuesday and Friday by [[Maria Lopez]]. About 12 percent of café sales are still cash; the rest is card and app.

Refunds over 25 need a manager. Staff drinks are free during a shift and are rung through as staff comps so the stock counts in [[Inventory Management]] stay honest.
