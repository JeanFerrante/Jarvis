# Quality Control Cupping

Thursday at 9:00 we cup the week's roasts. Three cups per batch, SCA protocol, scored out of 100. Anything under 82 is pulled from retail and goes into the café's batch brew instead.

[[Priya Shah]] leads, and at least one barista from the café joins so the front of house knows what is on the shelf. New hires cup within their first two weeks, see [[Onboarding Checklist]].

Results feed the [[Roast Log Standards]] and the tasting notes printed on the bags for the [[Subscription Program]].
