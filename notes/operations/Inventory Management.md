# Inventory Management

Green coffee is counted every Monday after roasting. We aim to hold eight weeks of green stock; less than four weeks triggers an order with [[Andes Green Coffee Importers]] or [[Kivu Direct Trade]].

Milk is ordered daily from [[Valley Dairy Co-op]], oat milk weekly. Retail bags, labels and valves come from [[Packaging Supplier]], ordered every six weeks.

Café consumables (cups, lids, napkins) are counted Friday. The par levels live in the inventory sheet; [[Maria Lopez]] owns it. Waste is logged daily, which is how we found that we were throwing away 9 percent of pastries, see [[Bakery Partnership]].
